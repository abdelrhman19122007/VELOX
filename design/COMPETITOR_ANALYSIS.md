# Competitor analysis

**Owner:** Hoda (@hodayasser511-stack) — UI/UX · October 2026

We looked at the delivery apps people in Egypt already use, kept what makes them familiar, and changed what doesn't
fit a small town like Samannoud.

## What we took
- **Four-tab layout** (Home · Orders · Cart · Account) — people already know where things are.
- **Store cards that answer the real questions**: is it open, how good is it, how long, how much is delivery.
- **One store per cart**, with a clear "start a new cart?" question.
- **Live tracking with steps and a map**, and a notification for every step.
- **Order again** on the home screen.
- **Offers row** with codes you can apply in one tap.

## What we changed for Samannoud
- Areas are **villages with fixed fees**, not distance-based pricing.
- The **landmark** is its own field and is highlighted for the driver ("جنب الجامع الكبير").
- **Errands** and **tuk-tuk-only large items** — common local needs the big apps don't handle.
- **Local payments** (Vodafone Cash code, Fawry reference) instead of cards.
- **No invented numbers** on the home page (no "500+ restaurants", no fake ratings): new stores show "جديد".
