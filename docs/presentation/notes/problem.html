The problem

Ordering food in Samannoud still runs on phone calls

FOR CUSTOMERS

  • No organized menus with real prices
  • Delivery fee unknown until the door
  • No idea where the order is
  • Mistakes from orders taken by phone

FOR RESTAURANTS

  • Orders scattered across calls and chats
  • Misheard items and addresses
  • No simple tool to update menus and prices
  • No courier network of their own

Our opportunity: one digital platform that makes local ordering clear, fast and organized.
