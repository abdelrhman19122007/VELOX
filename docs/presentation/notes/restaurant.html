Restaurant management

Giving local restaurants real tools

ON THE RESTAURANT’S PHONE

  • New orders ring, no refresh needed
  • Accept, reject with a reason, mark ready
  • See how long each order has waited
  • Change prices, pause sold-out items
  • Pause all orders when too busy

WHAT THEY GAIN

  • New customers in their own town
  • Clear written orders, fewer mistakes
  • A courier network they don’t have to hire
  • Works on any phone or tablet
