The order journey

From the kitchen to the doorstep

01

Order

Customer confirms after seeing the full cost

PLACED

02

Accept

Restaurant has 15 minutes, or it auto-cancels

ACCEPTED

03

Prepare

Kitchen cooks, then marks the order ready

READY

04

Pick up

A courier takes it, collects it from the shop

PICKED_UP

05

Deliver

Customer tracks it live until it arrives

DELIVERED

Every step reaches everyone instantly. Each move is allowed only to the right role, and an order no courier takes in 40 minutes is cancelled.

VELOX

Presenter: Raheek. This is a state machine: OrderStatus.canMoveTo checks the next status and who is asking, so a customer can’t accept their own order and a courier can’t pick up before READY. Updates travel over Server-Sent Events, with Firebase push when the app is closed. StaleOrderCloser runs every minute for the 15 and 40 minute rules.
